Plants make their own food. This process is called photosynthesis. It takes place mostly in the leaves of a plant.

Leaves contain a green substance called chlorophyll. Chlorophyll captures energy from sunlight. The leaf also takes in carbon dioxide from the air through tiny holes called stomata.

The plant uses sunlight, water and carbon dioxide to make glucose, a type of sugar. Oxygen is released into the air as a by-product. Animals and humans breathe in this oxygen.

Photosynthesis is important because it gives food to almost all living things. It also keeps the amount of oxygen and carbon dioxide in the air balanced.
